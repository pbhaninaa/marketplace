# Support guide

How to help buyers and providers as a member of the Agri Marketplace support team.

## At a glance

- Your **dashboard** shows an overview and links to your tools.
- **Users** helps you find a buyer's or provider's account.
- **Tickets** is where support requests are tracked.
- **Client OTP** resends a sign-in code to a buyer who didn't receive one.
- **Order invoice** looks up any order's invoice.

## Your account

Your administrator creates your account and gives you a temporary password. Sign in and change it straight away from **Profile**. Passwords must be 8 to 64 characters, not common, and not contain your email name.

Wrong password 5 times locks your account for 15 minutes; a password reset unlocks it.

## Finding a user

Open **Users** and **search by email**. Check:

- whether the account is a **buyer**, **provider owner**, **provider staff** or other role
- whether the account is **enabled**
- for providers: the business they belong to

Never ask a user for their password, and never share another user's details.

### Seeing what a provider sees

For a provider account, press **Shadow provider** to open the app as that provider and see exactly what they see. Your **Profile** is read-only while you do this.

When you're done, open the menu and press **Return to my support account**.

## Tickets

Open **Tickets** to see support requests with their **status**, **subject** and **created** date. Press **Refresh** to load new tickets. Follow up with the user, and record the outcome in your team's usual place.

## Resending a buyer's sign-in code

Buyers sign in with a code sent to their email or phone. If one didn't arrive:

1. Check that the buyer is using the **same email or phone** they used at checkout.
2. Ask them to check their **spam folder** and wait a minute.
3. Open **Client OTP**, enter their email or phone, and press **Resend OTP**.

Codes expire after **10 minutes**.

## Looking up an order invoice

Open **Order invoice** and enter the **order reference**. Confirm you are speaking to the buyer on the order before sharing anything from it.

## Common questions from buyers

**"An item disappeared from my cart."**
That seller stopped taking orders (for example their plan ended). Only that seller's items were removed; the rest of the cart is unchanged.

**"I have items from two sellers. Why do I pay twice?"**
Each seller is paid and delivers separately. After the first checkout the next seller's checkout opens with their details already filled in.

**"What is my verification code for?"**
The buyer shows it to the seller when collecting or receiving the order, so the seller knows the order is theirs.

## Common questions from providers

**"My listings aren't showing."**
Their trial or plan has probably ended. Ask them to open **Subscription** and choose a plan. If there is a good reason for more time, pass the request to the platform owner, who can extend a trial.

**"My staff member can't open orders."**
The owner or a team Admin must give them orders permission in **Team**. Viewers can only look.

**"I'm locked out."**
After 5 wrong passwords the account locks for 15 minutes. **Forgot password** on the sign-in page unlocks it.
