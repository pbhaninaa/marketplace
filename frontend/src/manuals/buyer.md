# Buyer guide

How to find, buy and rent livestock, vehicles, equipment and farm supplies on Agri Marketplace. You do not need an account to buy.

## At a glance

- **Browse** everything on the Marketplace page. Switch between **Buy** and **Rent** at the top.
- **Add items from as many sellers as you like.** Your cart keeps each seller's items together.
- **Check out one seller at a time.** Each seller is paid separately and delivers separately. Your details are remembered between sellers.
- **Keep your verification code.** You show it to the seller when you collect or receive your order.

## Finding what you need

1. Open **Marketplace** from the menu (or the logo at the top).
2. Choose **Buy** for items for sale, or **Rent** for equipment and vehicles you hire by the hour, day or week.
3. Narrow the list with the **Filters** panel:
   - **Category**, for example Livestock or Equipment
   - **Provider** (the seller)
   - **Min price** and **Max price**
   - **Location**, for example a town or region
   - **Search** words in the title or description
4. Press **Apply filters**. Press it again after changing a filter.

On a phone, tap **Filter** to open the filters.

> Only sellers who can take orders right now are listed, so anything you see can be ordered.

## Adding items to your cart

**Items for sale**

1. Set the **Quantity** on the listing card. The card shows how many are in stock.
2. Press **Add to cart**.

Some large livestock is sold one at a time, so the quantity stays at 1.

**Items for rent**

1. Choose the **start date** and **end date** on the listing card. Both days are included, and the rental can start today but not in the past.
2. Press **Add to cart**. The price is worked out from the seller's hourly, daily or weekly rate for those dates.

Each rental item can only be booked **once for any day**. The listing card shows the dates that are **already booked**. If your dates overlap one of them, the card tells you and **Add to cart** stays off until you pick other dates. Booking right after someone else (starting the day after their rental ends) is fine.

A rental is always **one item** for the dates you choose, so there is no quantity. To hire the same item for another period, add it again with the other dates.

The **cart button** at the top right shows how many items you have. Press it at any time to open your cart.

## Your cart

Your cart shows **one card per seller**, each with:

- the seller's name and location
- that seller's items, with quantities and prices
- a **Subtotal** for that seller
- a **Checkout** button for that seller

You can change the quantity of an item for sale with **−** and **+** (rentals show their dates instead), or remove an item with the bin icon. **Clear cart** removes everything.

Dates in your cart are **not reserved until you check out**. If someone else books the same dates first, checkout tells you, and you can choose other dates.

When your cart has more than one seller, the bottom of the page shows the **total for all sellers**. Delivery fees are added per seller at checkout.

## Checking out

Press **Checkout** on the seller you want to pay first. The checkout page shows only that seller's items and options.

1. Enter your **name**, **email** and **phone number**. South African numbers can be written as `082 123 4567` or `+27 82 123 4567`.
2. Choose how to **receive** your order:
   - **Pickup**: collect it from the seller.
   - **Delivery** (if the seller offers it): enter the distance in kilometres. The fee is the distance × the seller's rate per km, and is shown before you pay.
3. Choose how to **pay**. Each seller decides which methods they accept:

   | Method | What happens |
   |---|---|
   | **Cash on collection / delivery** | Pay the seller in person when you collect or receive the order. |
   | **EFT (bank transfer)** | The seller's bank details are shown. Pay the seller directly, using the reference shown. |
   | **Card / Instant EFT** | You are sent to a secure payment page (PayFast or Peach Payments) to pay. You come back to the marketplace afterwards and the page confirms your payment, usually within a few seconds. |

4. Check the **Subtotal**, **Delivery** and **Total**, then press **Place order**.

### More than one seller

After you place an order, you go straight on to the **next seller's checkout**. Your name, email and phone are already filled in; you only choose delivery and payment again, because those are different for every seller.

If you paid by card or Instant EFT, the page you return to says which sellers are still in your cart. Press **Continue to next seller**.

## Your verification code

Every order and rental booking gets a **verification code**. It is shown when you place the order and in **My orders**.

When you collect your order or it is delivered, **show the code to the seller**. They enter it to confirm the order is yours and has been paid. Do not share it with anyone else.

## Signing in to see your orders

You do not need a password. Use your email address or phone number:

1. Open **My orders** from the menu.
2. Enter the **email or phone number** you used at checkout and press continue.
3. Enter the **code** you receive by email or SMS, and press **Verify**.

You can then see all your orders and payments, with filters for status, payment and dates.

## Finding an order invoice

Open **Order invoice** from the menu. Enter your **order reference** and the **email address** you used at checkout. Both must match before the invoice is shown, so nobody else can open your invoice.

## Order statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting payment** | The order is placed but payment has not been confirmed yet. |
| **Paid** | The seller has confirmed your payment. |
| **Collected** | You have collected the order or it has been delivered. |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled and will not go ahead. |

## Settings and appearance

Open **Settings** from the menu to choose the **Light** or **Dark** theme. You can also switch it with **Dark mode** at the bottom of the menu.

Once you are signed in, the bottom of the menu also has a **Glass** switch for an alternative display style.

These choices are saved on your device.

## Questions and problems

**An item I added has disappeared from my cart.**
The seller has stopped taking orders, for example because their plan ended. Only that seller's items are removed; your other items stay in your cart.

**I can't add an item to my cart.**
It may be out of stock, or (for rentals) already booked for those dates. The message tells you which.

**I didn't receive my sign-in code.**
Check your spam folder, wait a minute and request a new code. Codes expire after 10 minutes, and you can only request a few codes in a short time.

**How long does an unpaid order stay open?**
Unpaid orders are cancelled automatically so the items go back on sale: card or Instant EFT payments after **1 hour**, EFT bank transfers after **3 days**, and cash orders after **14 days**. An unpaid rental is also cancelled once its rental dates have passed.

**I paid by EFT. What now?**
Keep your proof of payment. The seller confirms the payment, and your order changes to **Paid**.

**Who do I contact about an order?**
Contact the seller first: their name and location are on your order. For anything else, see **Help** in the menu.
