# Provider guide

How to sell and rent out your products on Agri Marketplace: signing up, your free trial and plan, listings, orders, payments, your team and your settings.

## At a glance

- **New providers get a 14-day free trial.** Your listings are visible and you can take orders straight away.
- **After the trial, choose a plan** (Basic or Premium) to keep selling. Without an active trial or plan your listings are hidden from buyers.
- **Buyers can order from several sellers at once**, but each seller's order, payment and delivery is separate. You only ever see and fulfil your own orders.
- **Every order has a verification code.** Ask the buyer for it when they collect or receive the order, and enter it to confirm.

## Signing up

1. Open **Sign up** (from the sign-in page, or the menu).
2. Fill in your **business name**, a **description** of what you offer, your **location** and your **provider type**:
   - **Reseller**: you mainly sell items.
   - **Renting owner**: you mainly rent out equipment or vehicles.
3. Enter the **owner email** and press **Email me a code**. Enter the 6-digit code from the email. This confirms the address is yours.
4. If another business invited you, enter their **referral code** (it may already be filled in from their link).
5. Choose a **password**: 8 to 64 characters, not a common password, and not containing the part of your email before the @.
6. Press **Create account**, then sign in.

## Your free trial and plan

Your **14-day free trial** starts when you sign up. The dashboard shows how many days are left.

Before the trial ends, open **Subscription** and choose a plan:

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | R199 per month | Dashboard, listings, orders, subscription and billing, business settings |
| **Premium** | R499 per month | Everything in Basic, plus team management, payroll, staff payments and priority support |

Pay online with **Card** or **Instant EFT** on the secure payment page (PayFast or Peach Payments). A monthly plan runs for **30 days** from payment. Renew before it ends to keep your listings visible.

> If your trial or plan ends, your listings are hidden from the marketplace until you choose a plan. Any of your items already in buyers' carts are removed from their carts.

## Your dashboard

The dashboard is a quick overview for the date range you choose:

- **Revenue** from sales and rentals (cancelled orders are not counted)
- **Purchase orders** and **Rental bookings** placed
- your **plan status**, for example *Free trial · 12 days left*
- a **Revenue** chart over time and the **Order status** mix

It updates by itself when new orders or payments arrive. For full details use **Orders** and **Order & payment history** in the menu.

## Listings

Open **Listings** from the menu.

**Adding a listing**

1. Press **Add listing**.
2. Choose **For sale** or **For rent**, and the **category**.
3. Enter a clear **title** (2 to 200 characters) and **description**.
4. For items for sale: enter the **price** and the **stock quantity** you have.
5. For rentals: choose which **rate types** you offer (hourly, daily, weekly) and enter each rate.
6. Add **photos**. Good, clear photos sell faster.
7. Save. The listing appears on the marketplace while it is **active** and your trial or plan is active.

**Keeping listings up to date**

- Edit a listing at any time to change price, stock, photos or description.
- Turn a listing **inactive** to hide it without deleting it.
- When stock reaches zero (after reserved orders), the listing is hidden until you add more stock.

## Orders and bookings

Open **Orders** from the menu to see purchase orders and rental bookings.

**Order statuses**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting payment** | The buyer placed the order. Stock is reserved but not yet deducted. |
| **Paid** | Payment is confirmed. |
| **Collected** | The buyer collected the order, or it was delivered. |
| **Cancelled** | The order will not go ahead. Reserved stock is released. |

**When the buyer collects or receives the order**

1. Ask the buyer for their **verification code**.
2. Open the order and enter the code. If it matches, the order is confirmed.
3. Mark the order **Paid** and then **Collected** as appropriate. For card and Instant EFT payments, the order is marked paid automatically once the payment provider confirms the payment.

If the code does not match, do not hand over the order. Check the order number with the buyer.

**Unpaid orders are released automatically**

So stock and rental dates are never held forever, orders that stay **Awaiting payment** are cancelled automatically (reserved stock is returned and you and the buyer are notified):

| Buyer chose | Cancelled if still unpaid after |
|---|---|
| Card / Instant EFT | 1 hour |
| EFT (bank transfer) | 3 days |
| Cash | 14 days |

An unpaid rental is also cancelled once its rental dates have passed. Mark orders **Paid** as soon as you receive payment.

**Rental bookings never overlap**

Each rental item can only be booked once for any day. A booking holds its dates while it is **awaiting payment**, **paid** or **collected** (out on hire). Only **cancelling** a booking frees its dates, so cancel bookings that won't go ahead. If a buyer cancels a card or Instant EFT payment on the payment page, their booking is cancelled automatically and the dates become free again.

Buyers see the already-booked dates on your listing and cannot book over them, even if two buyers try at the same moment.

**Payments by EFT**

When a buyer pays by EFT, they pay into your bank account using the details in your settings. Check that the money has arrived before marking the order **Paid**.

## Order and payment history

Open **Order & payment history** to see every order and payment, with filters for status, payment status, payment method, type and dates. Use **Export** to download the filtered list as **CSV** or **Excel**.

### Online payments and payouts

Card / Instant EFT payments are collected by the platform, not paid into your account straight away. The platform pays your share into the bank account in your settings, less the platform fee. The **Online payments and payouts** section at the top of the history page shows, for each online payment, what the buyer paid, the platform fee, what you get and the payout status:

| Payout status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting payout** | The platform holds your share and will pay it out. |
| **Paid out** | Paid to your bank account. The date and bank transfer reference are shown. |
| **Refund due to buyer** | The order or booking was cancelled after the buyer paid, so the buyer is refunded instead. |
| **Refunded to buyer** | The buyer was refunded. |

Cash and manual EFT are paid to you directly and carry no platform fee.

## Your team (Premium)

Open **Team** to add staff who help run your business.

**Roles**

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Almost everything the owner can, including team and business settings. |
| **Staff** | Day-to-day work, limited to the permissions you give them. |
| **Viewer** | Look only. Viewers can never change anything, even if given write permissions. |

**Permissions** decide what each person can see or change: listings (read or write), orders (read or write), rentals (read or write) and team (read or manage). Staff without order permissions cannot open orders.

For each team member you can also set a **pay rate** (per order, hour, day, week or month), a **target** and a **bonus %**, used by **Staff payments**.

## Staff payments (Premium)

Open **Staff payments** to see what each team member has earned from the orders they completed, and record payouts per order or pay everyone at once. Staff see their own expected income on the same page.

## Business settings

Open **Settings** to update:

- your **location** and **description**
- **Delivery**: whether you deliver, your **price per km**, and your **pickup location** on the map (where deliveries start). Delivery is only offered to buyers once all three are set. The fee is worked out automatically from your pickup point to the buyer's pin (straight line × 1.3 for roads); buyers can't change it. Delivery orders show the buyer's address and a **Get directions** link to their pin.
- the **payment methods** you accept: Cash, EFT (bank transfer) and Card / Instant EFT
- your **bank details** for EFT payments (bank, account name, account number, 6-digit branch code, reference)

Only the **owner** or a team **Admin** can change settings and bank details.

**Danger zone**: **Deactivate account** closes your business account. Use it only if you are leaving the marketplace.

## Referrals

Open **Referrals** to find your **referral code** and **invite link**.

When a business signs up with your code and pays for its **first subscription**, you earn a **commission** on that payment (15% unless the platform changes it). The page shows who you invited, who has qualified, and what you have earned and been paid.

A business can add a referral code later from their own Referrals page, as long as it is within the **claim window** (30 days unless changed) and before their first payment. You cannot refer your own business.

## Signing in and security

- **Wrong password 5 times** locks your account for **15 minutes**. Resetting your password unlocks it.
- Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page to get a reset link by email.
- Change your password from **Profile**.
- Never share your password. Each team member should have their own account.

## Questions and problems

**My listings are not showing on the marketplace.**
Check that your trial or plan is active (dashboard or **Subscription**), the listing is **active**, and it has stock.

**A buyer says their item vanished from their cart.**
Your trial or plan may have ended. Choose a plan; your listings reappear immediately.

**A staff member gets "Access denied" on orders.**
Give them the orders read (and, if needed, write) permission in **Team**. Viewers can only look.

**I can't change my bank details.**
Only the owner or a team Admin can.
